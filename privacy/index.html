Privacy notice

What happens to your ticket.

This notice explains how Instant Funding Today uses the information you submit through the business funding application on this website.

What we collect

Only what you enter into the ticket: details about your business, its owner and its finances, your requested amount, and how to contact you. A Social Security number is never requested. The business tax ID and the owner's home address are optional. We also record the page the ticket was submitted from.

How we use it

  • To review your funding request.
  • To contact you about that request, using the phone number or email you provided, because you consent when you submit.

What stays in your browser

This website does not save what you type into the ticket in your browser's storage. It is sent to us over an encrypted connection when you press send. Your browser or password manager may offer to autofill some fields on its own.

Submitting is not an agreement

Sending a ticket is a request for review. It does not guarantee approval or funding, and it does not commit you to any terms.

Questions, corrections and removal

To ask a question or request a correction or deletion of what you submitted, email info@instantfundingtoday.net or call (646) 807-8183.
