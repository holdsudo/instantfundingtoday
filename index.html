The product, in plain English

What a merchant cash advance actually is.

A merchant cash advance is a business funding product that is typically repaid through future business receivables. It is built around the revenue your business takes in, rather than a fixed monthly payment schedule.

Terms, delivery and eligibility depend on the details of the business and the request. That is why the ticket asks about revenue, deposits and existing financing.

Before you sign anything, ask for the total amount you will repay, how repayment is collected, and any fees, in writing.

Questions

The straight answers.

Does submitting the ticket guarantee approval or funding?

No. Submitting is a request for review. Approval, amount, terms and timing depend on the business and are never guaranteed.

Is submitting an agreement?

No. Sending a ticket asks us to review your request and contact you. You are not agreeing to any funding terms by submitting it. Read every term in full before you agree to anything.

Do you ask for a Social Security number?

No. The ticket does not ask for one. The business tax ID and the owner's home address are optional, and we may ask for them later if your request moves forward.

What's a merchant cash advance?

A business funding product that is typically repaid through future business receivables. Terms, delivery and eligibility depend on the details of the business and the request.

How do you use what I submit?

To review your funding request and to contact you about it. See the privacy notice for details.
