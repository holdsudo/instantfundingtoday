The route

What happens to a request.

Four stops. You are in control at every one of them, and nothing is binding until you agree to terms in writing.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Five short parts: the amount and purpose, the business, the owner, current finances, and a final review. Tax ID and home address are optional.

  2. 02

    Review

    Your request is read against what you reported for revenue, deposits and existing financing. Complete requests move through review more smoothly.

  3. 03

    Follow-up

    Because you consent when you submit, we contact you about your request using the phone number or email you provided.

  4. 04

    Your decision

    Read every term in full before you agree to anything. Submitting a request is not an agreement and carries no obligation.

The capital

Merchant cash advance, in plain English.

A merchant cash advance is a business funding product that is typically repaid through future business receivables. It is built around the revenue your business takes in, rather than a fixed monthly payment schedule.

Terms, delivery and eligibility depend on the details of the business and the request. That is why the request asks about revenue, deposits and existing financing.

Before you sign anything, ask in writing for

  1. 01The total amount you will repay.
  2. 02How repayment is collected, and how often.
  3. 03Every fee, and when it applies.

Questions

Direct answers.

Does submitting a request guarantee approval or funding?

No. Submitting is a request for review. Approval, amount, terms and timing depend on the business and are never guaranteed.

Is submitting an agreement?

No. Sending a request asks us to review it and follow up with you. You are not agreeing to any funding terms by submitting. Read every term in full before you agree to anything.

Do you ask for a Social Security number?

No. The request does not ask for one. The business tax ID and the owner's home address are optional, and may be requested later if your request moves forward.

What is a merchant cash advance?

A business funding product that is typically repaid through future business receivables. Terms, delivery and eligibility depend on the details of the business and the request.

Is the draft at the top of the page real?

No. It is a specimen, shown for illustration. It is not a negotiable instrument and not an offer or promise of funds. It simply starts your request.

How is what I submit used?

To review your funding request and to follow up with you about it. See the privacy notice for details.

What happens after I submit?

Your request is reviewed, and you are contacted using the phone number or email you provided. Keep the reference number shown on screen after you submit.
